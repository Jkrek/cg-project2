SKYPORT ARCHIPELAGO

Project 2: Cube World · CS5160 Computer Graphics I · Jared Krekeler
A volcanic island chain built from one 2×2×2 cube, drawn hundreds of thousands of times. Heights and colors are sampled from two PNG images; jets and a rocket fly Bézier curves under a ringed gas giant, through a full day/night cycle. WebGL 2, no libraries. Source
